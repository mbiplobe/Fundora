# Fundora

Fundora is an open-source donation management platform that helps charities, non-profit organizations, and community initiatives collect, manage, and distribute donations with transparency and accountability.

## Features

* Collect donations from individual and corporate donors
* Manage fundraising campaigns and beneficiaries
* Track donation and expense transactions
* Maintain a complete audit trail for independent auditing
* Allow donors to monitor how their donations are utilized
* Generate reports for donations, expenses, and campaign performance
* Support transparent fund distribution and accountability
* Modern API-first architecture for easy integration

## Transparency & Trust

Fundora is built with transparency as a core principle. Donors can track where their contributions are allocated and how funds are spent. Every financial transaction is recorded, creating a reliable history that can be reviewed by administrators, auditors, and stakeholders.

## Open Source

Fundora is an open-source project. Anyone can use, modify, and extend the platform to meet their organization's needs. Contributions from the community are welcome and encouraged.
